## Goal

Click "2D Goal Pose" in RViz. The rover plans a path, follows it, and stops.

## What you will learn

- **Global vs local planning**: one plans the whole route on the map, the other
  decides what to do in the next second
- Why **MPPI** samples many candidate futures and scores them, instead of
  following the line exactly
- What a **behaviour tree** is, and why this one has no Spin or BackUp

## Known state going in

- `config/nav2.yaml` is promoted but has **never been launched from this repo**.
- Planner: `NavfnPlanner`. Controller: MPPI, `vx_max` 0.30 m/s, `wz_max` 1.0.
- Both costmaps read `NvbloxCostmapLayer` at 5 cm, in `global_frame: odom`;
  `bt_navigator` uses `map`.
- Spin and BackUp plugins are loaded but the BT never calls them — deliberate on
  a tethered rover that cannot see behind itself.
- ⚠️ The collision monitor's obstacle source is stale (`TODO.md §3`), so
  `safety_guard.py` is the only real protection. **Settle that first.**

## Safety before this task

- Tether length checked, floor clear, hand on the power.
- `./rover.sh stop` is the e-stop; `./rover.sh down` is the harder one.
- Start with a goal **1 m away in open floor**, not across the room.
